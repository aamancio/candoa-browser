Talos

A browser workspace for the Mac. Native SwiftUI on Apple's WebKit, so it stays quiet, light on the battery, and out of your way.

Download for macOS macOS 14 or newer. All releases

What it does

SpacesWork, personal, a project, a rabbit hole. Each one keeps its own tabs, synced through iCloud.
Vertical and pinned tabsA sidebar you can read, with the tabs you keep at the top.
Keyboard firstOne command bar for addresses, search, and every action, with the shortcuts you already know.
Split viewTwo pages side by side when you need them.
Blocking built inAds and trackers stop at WebKit's content rules. No extension, no script watching you.
Chrome extensionsInstall straight from the Chrome Web Store. AI assistants such as Claude Code and Codex come this way.
PasskeysSign in with the passkeys in your Apple Passwords, confirmed with Touch ID.
Mini playerA video follows you to the next tab.

Honest notes

  • Talos is in beta and updates itself through Sparkle. Expect rough edges.
  • Nothing phones home. There are no accounts and no server behind the app; iCloud sync is Apple's.
  • DRM playback is unverified, so some streaming services may not work yet.
